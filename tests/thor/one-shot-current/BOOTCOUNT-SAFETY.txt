Thor OEM counter safety — source integration, 12 October 2026

The verified XV3-8/XE5-8 7.2-r1 OEM firmware contains Thor U-Boot
2016.01 v2.7.5a (15 June 2026). OEM container SHA256:
66a9b4bb558bf18a8e678d65ecf3857065683ba1da60885fa416d27a40f15192
Bootloader SHA256:
fd6ecad47036bf3135f4ecdd59b6ee9bfc57376f25b6fe745c0ff035ba2856da

Independent disassembly traces bootipq descriptor 0x4a973564 to handler
0x4a904118, then 0x4a9038b0 and helper 0x4a903308. The helper inspects
the first bootcount ASCII digit, flips image for 8/9, writes count1 and
persists it. This is not a general integer-threshold parser. A direct
RAM/UBI loader bypasses the helper, leaving a pending OEM alternate-bank
sentinel intact. Missing/mismatched selectors are not admitted.

Patch0189 uses shared0187 prepare_oem_return(running_slot) only in the
legacy one-OEM/one-OpenWiFi guard. Exact stock bootcmd and opposite OEM
selector are required. Counter0 write, sync and counter/image/bootcmd
readback must succeed before rearming or returning to OEM. Trial-prefix
environment writes and save must succeed before any candidate/native load.
If persistence fails, it performs no load or automatic reset; it returns
to the bootloader prompt. A reset could reload the old durable count9.
Native fallback after a successfully saved counter0/selector remains valid.
Converted A/B explicit FIT commands and their routing ABI are unchanged.

    python3 tests/thor/one-shot-current/test-oem-counter.py
